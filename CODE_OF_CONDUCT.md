# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be decent. Assume the person on the other side is competent and busy.

## Expected

- Technical criticism of the code, the metric model and the documentation, ours included. "This name is wrong and here is why" is a contribution.
- Reporting what you actually observed on your board, rather than what you assume happened.
- Accepting a "no" on scope. Some things are declined by design, and the reasons are written down in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the ADRs.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or dismissing someone instead of their argument.
- Posting a vulnerability publicly instead of following `SECURITY.md`.
- Posting someone else's fleet data or credentials in an issue. Device ids and site names often identify a real customer — redact before you paste.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **hola@opsvex.com**. The maintainers will read it, respond within 5 business days, and act — editing or deleting content, or blocking an account — as needed. A report stays confidential to the extent we can act on it.

Two people maintain this. Enforcement is proportionate and pragmatic, not procedural.
